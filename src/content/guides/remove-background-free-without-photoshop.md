## Free background removal in the browser

[Remove Background](/remove-background) uses an AI model that runs on your own device, so the photo is never uploaded.
It is free, without an account, a watermark or a limit on the number of photos.

1. Open [Remove Background](/remove-background) and add a JPG, PNG or WebP photo.
2. Choose a model. **Fast** (about 16 MB to download once) is quick and good for people. **Best quality** (about
   100 MB) does better on products, pets, objects and difficult edges.
3. Wait a few seconds. The first time, the model is downloaded; after that your browser keeps it.
4. Fix anything the AI got wrong with the brush, if needed (see below).
5. Save as a transparent PNG, or choose a solid color and save as PNG or JPG.

## Built-in options you may already have

**iPhone and iPad (iOS 16 and later).** In the Photos app, touch and hold the subject of a photo until it lights up,
then choose Copy or Share. You get the subject on a transparent background. It's very quick, but you can't fix the
edges or choose what counts as the subject.

**Mac (macOS Ventura and later).** Right-click an image in Finder and choose Quick Actions › Remove Background. Preview
also has Instant Alpha for removing areas of similar color by hand.

**Windows 11.** Recent versions of Paint have a Remove background button that cuts out the main subject in one click.

**PowerPoint.** Picture Format › Remove Background. It works for simple pictures, but you often have to mark the areas
to keep and remove by hand.

**Android.** Some phones' gallery apps, such as Samsung's, let you touch and hold a subject to lift it out.

**GIMP.** Free desktop software with selection tools that can cut out anything, but it takes practice and time.

These are fine for a quick cut-out. When the result needs to be clean, for a shop listing, a profile photo or a
design, a tool where you can check and fix the edges is worth the extra minute.

## Getting clean edges

Fine hair, fur, glass and subjects with colors close to the background are hard for any AI, free or paid. What helps:

- **Start with a good photo.** A subject that stands out clearly from the background, in even light, gives the
  cleanest result.
- **Try Best quality** when Fast misses parts of an object or leaves a halo.
- **Use the brush.** In Remove Background, choose "Erase, restore or keep with a brush":
  - **Erase** paints away leftover background.
  - **Restore** paints back parts the AI removed by mistake, such as strands of hair or the edge of a product.
  - **Keep** is for photos with several subjects: paint over the one you want, such as one person in a group, and
    everything not connected to it is removed.
- **Check on a color.** A transparent background hides faint halos. Choosing a dark or bright background color for a
  moment shows them, so you can erase them before saving.

## Logos, signatures and drawings on white

For a logo, an icon or a scanned drawing on a plain white background, an AI cut-out isn't the best choice: it looks for
a subject and can soften thin lines. [Transparent PNG Maker](/transparent-png-maker) instead makes exactly the white,
or any color you pick, transparent, and keeps edges sharp. For a handwritten signature, use
[Signature Extractor](/signature-extractor), which also ignores shadows and uneven light from phone photos.

## PNG or JPG?

- **PNG** keeps the transparent background. Use it for logos, stickers, designs and anything you'll place on top of
  something else.
- **JPG** can't be transparent, so the background is filled with a color, white unless you choose another. Use it for
  product photos on white and anything that just needs a plain background, since the files are smaller.

## Is it safe to use a free background remover?

Many free websites upload your photo to their servers to process it. For holiday snaps that may not matter; for
photos of children, ID documents or unreleased products, it might. Remove Background and the other TadaFile tools
process the photo on your own device, so it isn't sent anywhere. Only the AI model is downloaded.
